# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY
Self-driving cars are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to operate with limited or no continuous human control. They use technologies such as Machine Learning, Computer Vision, sensors, cameras, radar, and LiDAR to detect objects, recognize traffic signs, identify pedestrians, and understand road conditions. AI processes the collected data to determine the vehicle's position and make driving decisions. Path planning helps the car choose a suitable route, while obstacle detection helps it avoid collisions. Deep Learning enables the system to recognize patterns and improve object identification. Self-driving cars can automatically steer, accelerate, brake, and maintain a safe distance from other vehicles. These technologies aim to improve road safety, reduce human error, and provide convenient transportation. However, their safe operation depends on reliable systems, suitable road conditions, and proper testing.
# RESULT
The application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied successfully. The roles of Machine Learning, Computer Vision, sensors, and Deep Learning in detecting surroundings, making driving decisions, and navigating roads were understood. AI helps self-driving cars identify obstacles, follow routes, and control vehicle movements, demonstrating the importance of intelligent systems in the development of autonomous transportation.
