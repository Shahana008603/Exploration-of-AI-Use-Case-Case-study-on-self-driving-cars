# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY
Self-driving cars are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to operate with limited or no continuous human control. They use technologies such as Machine Learning, Computer Vision, sensors, cameras, radar, and LiDAR to detect objects, recognize traffic signs, identify pedestrians, and understand road conditions. AI processes the collected data to determine the vehicle's position and make driving decisions. Path planning helps the car choose a suitable route, while obstacle detection helps it avoid collisions. Deep Learning enables the system to recognize patterns and improve object identification. Self-driving cars can automatically steer, accelerate, brake, and maintain a safe distance from other vehicles. These technologies aim to improve road safety, reduce human error, and provide convenient transportation. However, their safe  operation depends on reliable systems, suitable road conditions, and proper testing.

## WORKING PRINCIPLE

Self-driving cars work using Artificial Intelligence (AI), cameras, sensors, radar, and LiDAR to detect and understand their surroundings. First, cameras and sensors collect information about roads, traffic signs, pedestrians, and nearby vehicles. Computer Vision and Machine Learning process this information to identify objects and road conditions. The AI system then makes decisions such as steering, accelerating, braking, and avoiding obstacles. Path-planning algorithms help the vehicle select a safe route and navigate toward its destination. The control system executes these decisions to manage the vehicle's movements. This process continues in real time, allowing the car to respond to changing traffic conditions and travel safely with minimal human intervention.

## Tools / Technologies Used
1. Cameras: Capture images of roads, traffic signs, vehicles, and pedestrians.
2. LiDAR: Uses laser pulses to measure distances and create a 3D map of the surroundings.
3. Radar: Detects nearby objects and measures their distance and speed.
4. Ultrasonic Sensors: Detect nearby obstacles during parking and low-speed movement.
5. Machine Learning and Deep Learning: Help identify objects, recognize patterns, and predict road users' movements.
6. GPS and HD Maps: Determine the vehicle's location and assist with route planning.
7. Computer Vision: Analyzes images to recognize traffic lights, road markings, and pedestrians.
8. Electronic Control Unit (ECU): Processes information and coordinates vehicle control systems.
9. Path-Planning Algorithms: Determine suitable routes and driving actions.
10. Actuators: Control steering, acceleration, and braking according to system commands.
11. Cloud Computing: Supports data storage, map updates, and system development in some vehicles.
12. V2X Communication: Allows compatible vehicles to exchange information with other vehicles and road infrastructure.

The application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied successfully. The roles of Machine Learning, Computer Vision, sensors, and Deep Learning in detecting surroundings, making driving decisions, and navigating roads were understood. AI helps self-driving cars identify obstacles, follow routes, and control vehicle movements, demonstrating the importance of intelligent systems in the development of autonomous transportation.
